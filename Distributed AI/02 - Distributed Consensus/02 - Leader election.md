Many problems in DAI and distributed systems require identifying a "coordinator" or a "leader".
We have 2 situations:
1. All processes / agents in the system are equally suitable for the role
2. Some processes / agents may be in a situation to act better than other for the role
These 2 situations are not substantially different from the algorithmic viewpoint
