ElectroMagnetic Interference (EMI) is a noise which affects any real-world signal.
There is a big difference between the noise affecting an analog signal and the one affecting digital signals.

In analog world, the data is the signal itself, however in a digital signal the data is a bunch of bits, so a bunch of levels.
The only possibility to avoid noise in an analog signal is to hope that the noise has a higher frequency than the signal itself, and apply a filter, while in a digital signal we are either noise-immune or not immune.
